# CM Legacy — Patches & Expansions Chronology

**Purpose:** Single timeline of **official updates**, **data disks**, **expansion SKUs**, and **community fixes** for CM1–CM4 and precursors.  
**Parent:** [CM_LEGACY_REFERENCE.md](CM_LEGACY_REFERENCE.md) · [CM_LEGACY_RESEARCH_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md](CM_LEGACY_RESEARCH_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md)  
**Date:** 2025-06-25

---

## How CM era handled “patches”

| Era | Model | Example |
|-----|-------|---------|
| **1992–1997** | **New SKU / data disk** | CM93 End-of-Season disk; CM2 European Leagues CD |
| **1997–1999** | Seasonal retail product | CM9798 replaces CM96/97 |
| **1999–2002** | Seasonal retail + covermount demos | CM3 → CM01/02 |
| **2003** | **Mandatory Enhancement Packs** | CM4 EP1 launch day → EP5 final |

Internet downloadable patches were **rare until CM4**; CM4’s broken launch **created** the Enhancement Pack culture SI continued into FM.

## CM4 (2003) — Enhancement Pack chronology

| EP | Version | Date | Highlights |
|----|---------|------|------------|
| **EP1** | 4.0.3 | **29 Mar 2003** (launch day) | Wages bug; rehab timeout; Swedish stadia; praise/criticise media |
| **EP2** | 4.0.4 | 11 Apr 2003 | Community “minimum playable” |
| **EP3** | 4.0.5 | ~May 2003 | Injury fixes; optional data update |
| EP4 | 4.0.7 | 2003 | Intermediate cumulative |
| **EP5** | **4.0.8** | **24 Jul 2003** | **Final** — NO DATA (~28 MB) or Updated Data (~46 MB) |

**After EP5:** No further CM4 patches — **CM Season 03/04** (Nov 2003) announced as successor/fix.

See [CM4_COMMUNITY_DEVELOPERS_AND_SUPPORT.md](CM4_COMMUNITY_DEVELOPERS_AND_SUPPORT.md) §2 for FTP mirrors.
