## 4. Core systems (CM3 base — detailed)

### 4.1 User interface **NEW**

- **Vertical sidebar** (left) + horizontal bars — still CM standard  
- High-resolution **context background images** per screen  
- Multitasking: browse squad while match engine processes  

### 4.2 Training **NEW**

- **Customisable training schedules** by squad group  
- Separate groups: first team, defenders, reserves, injured, etc.  
- Assistant can arrange **reserve/youth friendlies**  
- CM03/04 adds visual training indicators on player profiles (incremental)

### 4.3 Tactics **NEW depth**

- **Per-player instructions:** man-marking, forward runs, pressing, free roles  
- Set-piece takers retained from CM97/98  
- More cup competitions → more tactical variety needed

### 4.4 Youth & reserves **NEW**

- **Realistic reserve and youth squads**  
- **Trialists** system  
- Assistant delegation for youth/reserve management (expanded CM4 with leagues)

### 4.5 Scouting **NEW**

- Improved scouting vs CM2  
- CM99/00 adds **assign scouts** to players/teams/youth regions

### 4.6 International management **NEW**

- **Full national team career** — World Cup, Euros, Copa América pathways  
- CM97/98 had U21; CM3 expands senior international

### 4.7 Multiplayer **NEW**

- **LAN up to 16 players**  
- **Internet** play via LAN tunneling  
- **Hotseat expanded to 16** on one PC

### 4.8 Transfers & economy

- Global transfer market across 15 nations  
- CM99/00: **fine players**, **stadium upgrades**, board wage structure  
- CM00/01: **press conferences**  
- CM01/02: **EU regulated transfer windows**, **attribute masking (fog of war)**

### 4.9 Board & media

- Board expectations and budgets (deepened each seasonal update)  
- CM00/01 **media Q&A** — choose responses on form, signings  
- Player personality conflicts (00/01)

### 4.10 Save / hardware

- Save games ~**50 MB after 2 seasons** ⚠️ My Abandonware  
- Minimum: Pentium II, **64 MB RAM**  
- CM00/01 all leagues: ~**12 min first load** on period hardware ⚠️

## 12. CM3 → CM4 transition (2003)

Problems CM4 intended to fix:

- Text-only match feedback insufficient for tactics testing  
- Training "folklore" → CM4 makes training visually accountable  
- Media/board depth → CM4 fan feedback + praise/shame  

CM3 engine **maxed out** at 100 leagues / 100k players — CM4 rewrite for 2D + UI skins.
