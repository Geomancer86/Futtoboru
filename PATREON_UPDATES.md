## 🎨 Writing Guidelines

### Tone
- **Friendly & Engaging:** Write as if talking to friends who are excited about the project
- **Transparent:** Be honest about challenges and progress
- **Enthusiastic:** Show passion for the project
- **Professional but Accessible:** Technical but understandable

### Content Tips
1. **Start with Wins:** Lead with what's working and what's new
2. **Show Progress:** Use percentages, screenshots, or demos when possible
3. **Be Honest:** If something is delayed, explain why
4. **Engage:** Ask questions, invite feedback
5. **Visuals:** Screenshots, GIFs, or videos are highly valued
6. **Technical Depth:** Some supporters want technical details, others don't - balance both

### Length
- **Short Updates:** 200-400 words (weekly)
- **Detailed Updates:** 500-800 words (bi-weekly or monthly)
- **Major Milestones:** 800-1200 words (releases, major features)

### Sections to Always Include
1. What's New (wins)
2. Current Work (transparency)
3. Progress (metrics)
4. What's Next (roadmap visibility)
5. Thank You (appreciation)

## 🎯 Update Schedule

### Recommended Frequency
- **Weekly:** Short updates (200-400 words)
- **Bi-weekly:** Detailed updates (500-800 words)
- **Monthly:** Comprehensive updates (800-1200 words)

### Best Days to Post
- **Monday:** Start of week energy
- **Friday:** End of week summary
- **Mid-week:** If weekly, Wednesday works well
