## 🏛️ Executive Summary

The decade of 1910-1919 is a tale of two worlds. While Europe was paralyzed by **World War I (1914-1918)**, South America underwent a "Golden Age," culminating in the first **Copa América (1916)** and the founding of legendary Brazilian clubs like **Santos (1912)** and **Corinthians (1910)**. Technically, this decade introduces the final "Modern" tweak to the Goalkeeper—restricting their handling to the box (1912).

## 2. THE "FINAL MODERNIZATION" RULES

These rule changes effectively finish the "Laws of the Game" as we know them today.

### 2.1 The Goalkeeper Revolution (1912)
*   **The Rule:** Goalkeepers can no longer handle the ball outside the **Penalty Area**.
*   **Pre-1912 Logic:** GKs could handle the ball anywhere in their own half.
*   **Engine Impact:** Huge change to Match Simulator AI. GKs who stray too far will now be penalized (Red Cards/Handball).

### 2.2 The 10-Yard Rule (1913)
*   **The Rule:** Opponents must stand at least **10 yards** back from all free kicks.
*   **Engine Impact:** Standardizes set-piece visualization.

## 3. WWI MECHANICS: "THE GREAT SUSPENSION"

For the 1914-1918 period, Futtoboru must change its gameplay mode for European nations:

1.  **League Suspension:** Official leagues stop. No trophies are awarded.
2.  **Wartime Leagues:** Regional, smaller tournaments (e.g., "The Wartime League South") allow the game to continue for those not mobilized.
3.  **Player Mobilization:** Players may "disappear" from squads as they are called to the front. 
4.  **Memorial Events:** News scripts to honor famous players lost in the conflict (a powerful narrative element for the Community Manager).

## 4. MEDIA & COMMUNICATION (1910-1919)

### 4.1 The Wireless (Radio) Precursor
*   **Marconi's Wireless:** Results start to be transmitted via "Wireless Telegraphy." 
*   **Lag Reduction:** Intercontinental news (South America to Europe) can now arrive in **Real-Time** if it's "Breaking News," though most detailed reports still wait for the mail.

### 4.2 Wartime Journalism
*   **Propaganda & Morale:** Football reports were used to boost morale. Newspapers like *Athletic News* continued to publish, focusing on "Footballers at the Front."
*   **South America:** While Europe was at war, South American newspapers like *El Gráfico* (founded 1919) began to flourish, creating the first true "Sports Superstars."
