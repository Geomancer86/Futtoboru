## Cross-era sentiment arc

```
1992 CM1     modest sales · polarized reviews · “stats not names”
1993 CM93    real names · better scores · wait-time frustration
1995 CM2     franchise lock-in · European Leagues hunger
1997 CM9798  UK #1 · editor cult · last text-only peak
1999 CM3     golden community · PC Zone 93%
2003 CM4     biggest launch + biggest patch crisis → EP culture → FM era
```
