### Gameplay overlays vs English CM93 ⚠️ secondary

| Rule | Italia behaviour |
|------|------------------|
| Divisions | **2** (Serie A/B) — no Serie C |
| Foreign players | **Max 3 non-Italians on pitch** |
| Transfer windows | **Mercato** — restricted buy/sell periods vs England year-round |
| AI style | More defensive; lower scores (catenaccio era simulation) |
| Backgrounds | Italian stadium / kit flavour |

## Commercial / reception context ⚠️ secondary

- Combined Amiga CM + Italia sales cited ~**90,000** by early-90s (RetroAffaire)  
- Serie A TV boom (**Football Italia** Channel 4) drove UK demand for calcio management  
- **CM '94 Amiga** was last Amiga-native CM expansion before PC-centric **CM2**

## Futtoboru relevance

Spin-offs prove **mod-style national rule packs** predate Futtoboru:

- Foreign quota rules → future `competitions` rule modules  
- Transfer windows → season calendar constraints  
- Two-division pyramids → simpler than English 4-div CM1
