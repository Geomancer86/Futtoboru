# Data review 1888-89: research findings (WORKSTATION, 2026-11-27)

Hand-written companion to `REVIEW_1888-89.md` (generated by the now retired `convert_legacy.py`; it is a frozen
list now). Section names follow that file. The findings below were **applied to `gamedata/` in P2-4 slice 4**; see
"Applied" right below for what changed and what is still open. Each record also carries its source URL and a
`notes` line in the JSON.

Web research against Wikipedia season/club/player articles and the sources they cite. "Not found" means no online
source was found, not that the person is fake; several clubs need offline books or newspapers (listed at the
end). Many 11v11 / englishfootballleaguetables pages refused automated access, so no full 1888-89 line-ups for the
minor clubs were read.
