## History (game text)
Grimsby Town began as Grimsby Pelham in 1878, named after the Earl of Yarborough's family, and soon became the strongest club in Lincolnshire. In 1888-89 they played in the short-lived Combination and battled through four qualifying rounds, including three games with Lincoln City. They beat Sunderland Albion and then hosted Preston, the unbeaten champions, before leaving Clee Park for Abbey Park.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1878: formed as Grimsby Pelham [1]
- 1879: renamed Grimsby Town [1]
- 1880: buy land at Clee Park (one season at Lovett Street before returning) [1]
- 1884: change to chocolate and blue [1]
- 1888-89: The Combination; Cup run ends v Preston [1][3]
- 1889: League application refused; join the Football Alliance; move to Abbey Park [1][2]
- 1890: become a limited company [1]
- 1892: elected to the new Second Division; first game, 2-1 v Northwich Victoria [1][2]
