## History (game text)
The Old Carthusians gather the old boys of Charterhouse, one of the schools that shaped the passing game. They won the FA Cup in 1881, but their 1883 semi-final loss to the working men of Blackburn Olympic marked the end of the old boys' era. Their side still holds England internationals, among them the Walters brothers and William Cobbold, and in 1888-89 they pushed the League's Wolves all the way before losing 4-3.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1876: club formed [1]
- 1879-80: first FA Cup entry [1]
- 1881: FA Cup winners, beating Old Etonians [1]
- 1883: FA Cup semi-final, lost to Blackburn Olympic [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: lose 3-4 at Wolves in the FA Cup first round [2]
- 1894: win the first-ever FA Amateur Cup final, 2-1 v Casuals; decline the new Southern League [1]
- 1894-95: London Senior Cup winners [1]
