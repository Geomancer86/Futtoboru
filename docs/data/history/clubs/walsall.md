## History (game text)
Walsall Town Swifts were formed in early 1888 when the town's two senior clubs, Walsall Town and Walsall Swifts, joined forces at the Chuckery. Their first game was the Birmingham Charity Cup final against Aston Villa, and that summer they beat Burnley and held West Bromwich Albion in front of 7,000. In 1888-89 they finished third in the Midland Association and won an FA Cup tie before a heavy defeat at Wolves.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 9 Apr 1888: first match, 0-0 v Aston Villa in the Birmingham Charity Cup final (trophy given to Villa after a dispute over the replay venue) [1]
- 1888: friendlies: beat Burnley 1-0, 2-2 v West Bromwich Albion [1]
- 1888-89: Midland Association, 3rd [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: beat Sheffield Heeley 5-1 in the FA Cup [4]
- 1889: elected to the Football Alliance [1][6]
- 1892: founder member of the Football League Second Division [1]
- 1893: move to West Bromwich Road after residents complain [1]
- 1895: fail re-election; move to Fellows Park [1]
