## History (game text)
Forest were founded in 1865 by shinty players at the Clinton Arms and chose "Garibaldi red" after the Italian patriot's redshirts, the first club to wear red officially. They gave Arsenal a set of red shirts in 1886, and their best player, the England centre forward Tinsley Lindley, came home from Cambridge in 1888. The League refused to admit them, so 1888-89 is a season of friendlies and Cup ties, ending with an upset loss to Chatham after three games.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1865: club founded; colours set as Garibaldi red [1]
- 1874: Sam Weller Widdowson credited with inventing shin pads [4]
- 1878-79: first FA Cup, reach the semi-final, lost 1-2 to Old Etonians [2]
- 1885: move to the Gregory Ground; first game 26 Sep 1885, beat Stoke 4-0 [3]
- 1886: donate red kits to the new Woolwich Arsenal [1]
- 1888: League application rejected; Lindley rejoins [2][8]
- 28 Feb 1889: knocked out of the Cup by Chatham at the Oval [5]
- 25 Mar 1889: floodlit match v Notts Rangers at the Gregory Ground, lit by 14 Wells lights [3]
- 1889: join the Football Alliance [1]
- 1890: move to the Town Ground [1][3]
- 1892: Football Alliance champions; elected to the Football League; FA Cup semi-final [2]
