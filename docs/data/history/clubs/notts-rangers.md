## History (game text)
Notts Rangers grew from a church side, St James, into a club good enough to win the Nottinghamshire Senior Cup in 1888 and 1889. The FA made them one of the clubs exempt from qualifying in 1888-89, to some surprise, and they took The Wednesday to a replay. In the Combination they had the best record of any club that played its full programme, yet crowds were tiny next to Notts County and Forest, and their best players drifted away.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1880: renamed Nottingham Rangers [1]
- 1885-86: first FA Cup entry [1]
- 1886: move to Meadow Lane [1]
- 1887-88: best Cup run, including a 10-1 win over Jardines; Notts Senior Cup winners [1]
- 1888: Charlie Shelton wins the club's only England cap [1]
- 1888-89: Combination; Notts Senior Cup again; Cup exit to The Wednesday [1][2]
- 25 Mar 1889: lose to Forest in a floodlit match at the Gregory Ground [3]
- 1889-90: Midland League, win 1 of 15; club gone by 31 May 1890 [1]
