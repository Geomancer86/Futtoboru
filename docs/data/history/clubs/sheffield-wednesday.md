# The Wednesday (`sheffield-wednesday`)
Sheffield's leading club, newly professional and in a ground of its own, reaching the Cup quarter-finals without a league.

## History (game text)
The Wednesday grew out of a cricket club in 1867 and soon dominated Sheffield football, winning the first Sheffield Challenge Cup. In 1887 their best players threatened to walk out and form a professional Sheffield Rovers; the club gave in, turned professional, and built Olive Grove so it no longer had to share gates with Bramall Lane. Without a league in 1888-89, Wednesday made the FA Cup quarter-finals, beating Notts County along the way.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1867: founded; Feb 1868 win the Cromwell Cup [1]
- 1872: Charles Clegg plays in the first international [1]
- 1876: James Lang joins, regarded as the first professional; win the first Sheffield Challenge Cup [1]
- 1882: FA Cup semi-final; cricket and football clubs split [1]
- 22 Apr 1887: club turns professional; Olive Grove opens that September [1][3]
- 2 Mar 1889: FA Cup quarter-final, lost 0-3 at Wolves [4]
- 1889-90: Football Alliance champions; FA Cup final, lost 1-6 to Blackburn Rovers (Mumford scores) [1][6]
- 1892: elected to the Football League First Division, top of the poll [1]
- 1896: FA Cup winners, 2-1 v Wolves [1]
