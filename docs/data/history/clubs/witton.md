## History (game text)
Witton began as St Mark's, a cricket club's football side near Blackburn, and grew into a professional outfit that reached the Lancashire Cup semi-finals. Shut out of the Football League because Blackburn already had Rovers, the club joined the rival Combination and then quit it as second-rate. That left Witton playing friendlies, mostly away, in 1888-89, and suffering heavy defeats to Everton, Darwen and Preston, though they ran Aston Villa close in the Cup.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1874: founded as St Mark's [1]
- 1879-80: founder member of the Lancashire FA, in the first Lancashire Senior Cup [1]
- 1882-83: beat Stanley 23-0 in the Lancashire Cup [1]
- 1883: Jimmy Forrest leaves for Blackburn Rovers [1]
- 1887-88: turn professional; FA Cup third round; Lancashire Cup semi-final [1]
- 1888: Haresnape and Almond join Blackburn Rovers; club leaves the Combination [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: lose 2-3 at Aston Villa in the FA Cup first round [2]
- 1889: miss Football Alliance membership on the chairman's casting vote, which went to Long Eaton Rangers [1]
- 1890-91: revived Combination, resign mid-season; 1891-92 Lancashire League [1]
- April 1892: suspended by the FA over a 30-shilling debt; seems to stop playing [1]
