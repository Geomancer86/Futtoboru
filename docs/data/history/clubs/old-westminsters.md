## History (game text)
The Old Westminsters gather the former pupils of Westminster School, whose cloister football helped shape the passing game. Formed as a proper club in 1880, they reached three FA Cup quarter-finals in the 1880s and were usually the strongest side in the capital, but by 1888 the professionals of the north and Midlands had left the old boys behind. They remain a force in the London cups and won the London Charity Cup in 1888-89.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1880: formal Old Westminsters club formed [1]
- 1884: FA Cup quarter-final, lost 0-1 to Queen's Park at the Oval [1]
- 1886: FA Cup quarter-final, beaten 0-6 by West Bromwich Albion [1]
- 1887, 1888: London Senior Cup winners (1887 shared) [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: lose 3-4 at Burnley in the FA Cup first round [1][2]
- 1888-89: London Charity Cup winners [1]
- 1890, 1892, 1893: London Senior Cup winners [1]
- 1891-92: last FA Cup proper tie, 2-3 v West Bromwich Albion at the Oval (6-7,000 crowd) [1]
- 1893-94: last FA Cup entry, lost to Luton Town in the third qualifying round [1]
- 1894: declined to join the new Southern League [3]
