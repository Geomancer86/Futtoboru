## History (game text)
Derby Junction began as the old boys of Junction Street School and stayed firmly amateur. In 1887-88 they shocked the Cup holders Blackburn Rovers 2-1 on a frozen, bumpy pitch to reach the FA Cup semi-final, lost to West Bromwich Albion, and then became the first Derby side to win the Derbyshire Senior Cup. In 1888-89 they struggled in the Combination and lost a Derby derby to the League's County in the Cup.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1885: renamed Derby Junction Street [1]
- 1886-87: Birmingham Senior Cup quarter-final [1]
- 1887-88: beat Blackburn Rovers 2-1 (goals S. Smith, W. Hopkins); FA Cup semi-final, lost to West Bromwich Albion at Stoke [1]
- 1888: Derbyshire Senior Cup winners, 2-0 replay v Staveley [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: lose 0-1 at Derby County in the FA Cup first round [2]
- 1890: founder member of the Midland League [1]
- 1895: folded; last match 25 Dec 1895, lost 1-8 to Derby County reserves [1]

## Data notes
- Founded 1870 in `clubs.json` is the club's own claim; reports show play only from the early 1880s [1].
- Ground: `derby-junction-ground` should be the Arboretum Field, Derby [1].
- Squad: only S. Smith and W. Hopkins are sourced (1887-88 scorers); Radford and Bromage scored in the 1888 Derbyshire Cup final [1]. The rest are placeholders.
