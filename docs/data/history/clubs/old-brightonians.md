## History (game text)
The Old Brightonians are former pupils of Brighton College playing out of London. Their best FA Cup run took them to the third round in 1887-88, and in 1888-89, the first season of qualifying rounds, they won four ties to reach the first round proper, one of only two old-boy sides to do so. They went down 2-0 at Notts County, the first goal an own goal and the second at the death.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1884-85: first FA Cup entry, lost to Swifts with ten men [1]
- 1886-87: win 6-0 away at former Cup holders Clapham Rovers [1]
- 1887-88: best run, third round, lost to Old Carthusians [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: lose 0-2 at Notts County in the FA Cup first round [1][2]
- 1889-90: withdraw at the third qualifying round because players were needed for the Varsity Match [1]
- 1891-92: lose 2-3 to Chatham in a disputed third qualifying round tie [1]
- 1892-93: last FA Cup entry, lost to Old Westminsters [1]
