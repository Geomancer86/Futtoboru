## History (game text)
Heeley, a church club from the Sheffield suburb of the same name, was one of the leading sides of the Sheffield Rules era and played The Wednesday in the first Sheffield Challenge Cup final in 1877. Jack Hunter, later of Blackburn Olympic, won England caps here. By 1888 the professionals had passed the amateurs by, but Heeley still won four qualifying ties before losing at Walsall.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1862: founded [1]
- 1870: move from Wellsbrook Park to Meersbrook [1]
- 1877: lose the first Sheffield Challenge Cup final 3-4 to The Wednesday [1]
- 1881-82: FA Cup fourth round, the club's best; 1882 Sheffield Challenge Cup winners [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: lose 1-5 at Walsall Town Swifts in the FA Cup first round [2]
- 1889-90: last FA Cup run, lost to Sheffield United in the second qualifying round [1]
- c.1899: absorbed by Heeley Friends [1]
