## History (game text)
Small Heath Alliance started in 1875 on waste ground in Bordesley Green and settled at a rented field off the Coventry Road in 1877. They turned professional in 1885, reached the FA Cup semi-final in 1886, and in 1888 became the first football club to form a limited company with a board, dropping "Alliance" from the name. In 1888-89 they won three qualifying ties at home, scoring 16 goals, before losing narrowly to the Cup holders West Bromwich Albion.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1875: founded as Small Heath Alliance [1]
- 1877: move to the Muntz Street (Coventry Road) ground [2]
- 1885: turn professional [1]
- 1885-86: FA Cup semi-final [5]
- 1888: become Small Heath F.C. Ltd, the first club with a board of directors [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: lose 2-3 at home to West Bromwich Albion [3]
- 1889: join the Football Alliance [1]
- 1892: founder member of the Second Division; champions 1892-93 but lose the test match [1]
- 1894: promoted to the First Division after a test match win over Darwen [1]

## Data notes
- Founded 1875 and `muntz-street` match; the ground was called Coventry Road at the time [2].
- Squad checks: Chris Charsley (amateur goalkeeper and police officer, at the club 1886-94, England cap 1893) [6]; Caesar Jenkyns joined in 1888, later 8 Wales caps, nicknames "Jumbo" and "The Mighty Caesar" [7]; Ted Devey signed in 1888 [8]; Will Devey top scorer in 1889-90 and 1890-91 [9].
