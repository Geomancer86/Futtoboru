## History (game text)
The Swifts are a club of Berkshire and Buckinghamshire gentlemen who reached FA Cup semi-finals in 1874, 1876 and 1886. Charles Bambridge, the most capped of the three Bambridge brothers, made them famous, and the Scottish star Andrew Watson also turned out for them. They play at the Dolphin Ground in Slough and still beat good sides, but amateur football is shrinking around them.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1871: club founded; first game v Windsor Home Park, 0-0 [1]
- 1873: beat a depleted Wanderers 5-0 at the Oval [1]
- 1874, 1876, 1886: FA Cup semi-finals (lost to Royal Engineers, Wanderers, Blackburn Rovers) [1]
- 1887-88: beat Crewe 3-2 in the FA Cup, but Crewe's protest that a crossbar was too low forced a replay, which Crewe won [1]
- 1887, 1888: London Charity Cup winners; 1889 runners-up [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: beat Wrexham 3-1 in the FA Cup first round [2]
- 1891: scratched from the FA Cup and lost the Dolphin Ground to the Slough club [1]
- 1893: last FA Cup entry; last recorded game 27 Nov 1895 [1]
