## History (game text)
West Bromwich Albion grew out of a works team: in 1878 young men from Salter's spring factory began playing as the West Bromwich Strollers, and two years later took the name Albion from a district of the town. They tried a rainbow of colours before settling on blue-and-white stripes in 1885, the year they turned professional and moved to Stoney Lane.

Albion became the cup-fighters of the Midlands. They lost the FA Cup final in 1886 and 1887, then in March 1888 beat the great Preston North End 2-1 at the Kennington Oval, Jem Bayliss and George Woodhall scoring and 19-year-old Billy Bassett running the show. Almost the whole side was local, and its professionals earned about ten shillings a week.

As Cup holders they entered the first Football League season with most of that team intact: "Long Bob" Roberts in goal, the Perry and Horton brothers, Timmins, Pearson and Bassett.

## 1888-89 season
Albion began with a 2-0 win at Stoke, Joe Wilson scoring the club's first league goal late on, and a 5-0 home win over Derby (Pearson three) in early October. Defeats to Preston, home and away (0-3, 0-5 on Boxing Day), and a 1-5 home loss to Bolton showed the gap to the leaders, but Bassett and Pearson, 11 league goals each, kept them mid-table and they finished sixth [3][5]. The FA Cup was again the highlight: Walter Perry's hat-trick beat Burnley 5-1, and Albion won 10-1 at Chatham before 17,000, but the semi-final was lost 0-1 to Preston at Bramall Lane in front of 22,688 [3][4]. Roberts, Pearson and Timmins played every league game [3][6].

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1878: founded as West Bromwich Strollers [1]
- 1880: renamed West Bromwich Albion [1]
- 1883: first trophy, the Staffordshire Cup; joins the FA [1]
- 1885: turns professional; moves to Stoney Lane; record home crowd 16,393 v Blackburn Rovers in the FA Cup [1][2]
- 1886: FA Cup final, lost to Blackburn Rovers after a replay [1]
- 1887: FA Cup final, lost 0-2 to Aston Villa [1]
- 24 Mar 1888: FA Cup winners, 2-1 v Preston at the Oval (19,000) [4][7]; then lost 1-4 to Renton in a "World Championship" match [1]
- 17 Apr 1888: founder member of the Football League [1]
- 1891: Charlie Perry becomes captain [2]
- 1892: FA Cup winners, 3-0 v Aston Villa [1]
- 28 Apr 1894: Bassett becomes the first Albion player sent off, for "unparliamentary language" [2]
- 1895: FA Cup final, lost 0-1 to Aston Villa [1]
