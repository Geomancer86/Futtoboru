# Sunderland Albion (`sunderland-albion`)
A breakaway formed in March 1888 by Sunderland's own founder, James Allan, and Sunderland AFC's bitter rival.

## History (game text)
Sunderland Albion were born out of a row: when Sunderland AFC were thrown out of the 1887-88 Cup for fielding Scots under false names, the club's founder James Allan left and set up a rival, officially amateur, with jobs in local pubs and glassworks for its players. Twice in 1888-89 the cup draws paired the two Sunderland clubs, and twice Sunderland AFC withdrew. Albion won the Durham Challenge Cup in their first season, though they lost at Grimsby in the FA Cup.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 13 Mar 1888: club founded [1]
- 5 May 1888: first match, 3-0 v Shankhouse [1]
- Dec 1888: Sunderland AFC withdraw from the Cup tie; 14,000 watch a 2-0 Sunderland win in a friendly instead [1]
- Jan 1889: second derby friendly ends in a walk-off and stoning of Albion players [1]
- 1889: Durham Challenge Cup winners, 3-0 v Birtley; League application gets no votes [1]
- 1889-90: Football Alliance; disqualified from the FA Cup over two ineligible players [1]
- 1890-91: Alliance runners-up; League again picks Sunderland AFC instead [1]
- 11 May 1892: club wound up [1]
