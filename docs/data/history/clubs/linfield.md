## History (game text)
Linfield Athletic were formed in 1886 by workers at the Linfield Mill in Sandy Row and quickly gained a name for a passing game new to Irish football. In 1888-89 they knocked out a Football League side, Bolton Wanderers, in qualifying, beat Cliftonville on Christmas Day, and drew 2-2 at Nottingham Forest. Then they had to concede the tie over an ineligible player; the clubs kept it quiet and played the Belfast "replay" as a friendly, which Linfield won before a large, unaware crowd.

## Key moments to ~1895
- March 1886: club founded at the Linfield Mill [1]
- 17 Nov 1888: beat Bolton Wanderers 4-0 in the third qualifying round [1][3]
- 25 Dec 1888: beat Cliftonville 7-0, the only Christmas Day FA Cup tie [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: draw 2-2 at Nottingham Forest, then concede the tie [1][2]
- 1889: move to Ulsterville Avenue [1]
- 1890: Bob Milne joins from the Gordon Highlanders; founder member of the Irish League and first champions [1]
- 1890-91: last FA Cup entry, lost 4-5 to Nantwich [1]
- 1894: leave Ulsterville for housing development [1]
