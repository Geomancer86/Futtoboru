## History (game text)
Long Eaton Rangers formed in 1881 and were unbeaten at home for two seasons. Their high point came in 1887, when a late deflected free kick beat West Bromwich Albion in the Birmingham Senior Cup final at Wellington Road. The League judged the town too small, so the Rangers joined the Combination in 1888-89 and did well there. The FA Cup draw was unkind as usual, sending them to Birmingham St George's.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1881: founded [1]
- 1882-83: win 23 of 26 games [1]
- 1887: Birmingham Senior Cup winners, 1-0 v West Bromwich Albion (goal by Winfield) [1]
- 1888-89: The Combination; Derbyshire Charity Cup winners [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: lose 2-3 at Birmingham St George's [2]
- 1889: join the Football Alliance on the chairman's casting vote over Witton; finish bottom, lose 1-9 at The Wednesday [1]
- 1890-91: Midland League runners-up; Derbyshire Senior Cup winners [1]
- 1899: club folds [1]
