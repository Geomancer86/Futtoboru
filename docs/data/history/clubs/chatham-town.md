## History (game text)
Chatham are a young Kent club with army roots, wearing red and blue hoops. In 1888-89 they came through four qualifying rounds, then beat South Shore and, after three games, Nottingham Forest to reach the last eight, the best run of any southern amateur side that year. Their home was an open military field where no admission could be taken, so visiting clubs travelled for nothing; the complaints led the FA to require enclosed grounds, and Chatham moved to Maidstone Road in 1889.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1882: formed as Chatham United [1]
- 1885-86 to 1887-88: Kent Badge winners three years running [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: beat South Shore 2-1, whose protest over the gateless ground leads to the enclosed-ground rule [3]
- 28 Feb 1889: beat Forest 3-2 at the Oval in a second replay [2]
- 2 Mar 1889: lose 1-10 to West Bromwich Albion in the quarter-final [2]
- 1888-89: Kent Senior Cup winners [1]
- 1889: move to Maidstone Road, enclosed with a pavilion for £125 [1]
- 1894: founder member of the Southern League and the Kent League; Kent League champions 1894-95 [1]
