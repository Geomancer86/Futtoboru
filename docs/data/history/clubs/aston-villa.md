# Aston Villa (`aston-villa`)
The Cup holders of 1887 and home of William McGregor, the man who invented the League; a fast, short-passing Birmingham side.

## History (game text)
Villa were founded in 1874 by young men from a Wesleyan chapel cricket team in Aston, who wanted a winter game. In 1876 they took on a rented field at Perry Barr. That same year a Glaswegian, George Ramsay, happened on one of their practice games in Aston Park and was asked to join in. He became captain and taught the team the Scottish passing game.

Villa won the Birmingham Senior Cup in 1880, and with Archie Hunter as captain they won the FA Cup in 1887, beating West Bromwich Albion 2-0 at the Oval. They were the first Midlands club to win it. In March 1888 Villa committee man William McGregor wrote to the leading clubs proposing a league with fixed fixtures. The Football League was the result, and Villa were among its twelve founders.

## 1888-89 season
Villa's first League game was a 1-1 draw at Wolves on 8 September. Tommy Green scored, and Gershom Cox put through his own goal. They beat Stoke 5-1 in the first League match at Wellington Road, then thrashed Notts County 9-1, with Albert Allen scoring the club's first League hat-trick. On 1 October Archie Goodall arrived from Preston, the first in-season transfer the League approved. He scored 7 goals in 14 games. Villa won every home game until Preston won 2-0 at Perry Barr on 9 February, and finished second, a long way behind the Invincibles. Allen was top scorer with 19 in all competitions. The season ended badly: an 8-1 FA Cup thrashing at Blackburn and a 5-2 defeat at Derby. [7] [8] [9]

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1874: Club formed at Villa Cross (21 November) [2]
- 1876: Moves to Wellington Road; George Ramsay joins [1] [3]
- 1880: First honour, the Birmingham Senior Cup [1] [2]
- 1886: Ramsay appointed secretary (June); chocolate and sky blue proposed (November) [2] [4]
- 1887: FA Cup winners, 2-0 v West Bromwich Albion; Hunter scored in every round [1] [6]
- 1888: Record Wellington Road crowd, 26,849, v Preston in the FA Cup (7 January; Preston won 3-1; pitch invasion) [3]
- 1888: McGregor's letter (2 March) and the Football League founded (Manchester, 17 April) [5]
- 1890: Archie Hunter collapses with a heart attack during a League match v Everton and never plays again [6]
- 1892: Loses the FA Cup Final 0-3 to West Bromwich Albion; rumours (unproven) about goalkeeper Warner [11]
- 1894: First League title; Archie Hunter dies (29 November) [2] [6]
- 1895: FA Cup winners [2]
