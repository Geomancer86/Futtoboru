## History (game text)
Bootle began as a church team in 1879 and became Everton's main local rival. When the Football League formed, William McGregor invited Everton to represent Liverpool, so Bootle went into the Combination. Their great signing was Andrew Watson, three times capped by Scotland and widely seen as the first black international footballer, who joined in 1887. In the Cup they drew the unbeaten champions Preston.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1879: founded as Bootle St Johns [1]
- 1883-84: move to Hawthorne Road [1][2]
- 1887: sign Andrew Watson, probably the first black professional [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: lose 0-3 at home to Preston North End [3]
- 1889-90: founder member of the Football Alliance, runners-up; FA Cup quarter-final, lost 0-7 to Blackburn [1]
- 26 Dec 1889: 20,000 watch a friendly with Everton, the ground record [2]
- 1892-93: one season in the Second Division, 8th, then resign [1]
- 1893: club goes into liquidation (28 Aug) [1]
