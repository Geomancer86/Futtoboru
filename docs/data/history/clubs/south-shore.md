## History (game text)
South Shore are the senior club of Blackpool, paying players half-a-crown a match since 1885-86. That season they beat Notts County at home and reached the FA Cup quarter-finals. In 1888-89 they played in the short-lived Combination, then travelled to Chatham for the Cup, where the open field let the hosts charge no admission and the visitors got no share of a gate; their complaint led the FA to require enclosed grounds. Their 1889 bid for the Football League got no votes, and a new local club, Blackpool, began to overtake them.
