## History (game text)
Halliwell, from the north-west of Bolton, went professional early with the help of local mill owners and by 1885 had more Scottish players than almost anyone in England. They won the Bolton Charity Cup in 1887, but the League wanted only one Bolton club and chose the Wanderers. In 1888-89 they played in the Combination, thrashed Crewe in a Cup replay, and beat weakened Bolton and Burnley sides in the Lancashire Cup before losing to Birmingham St George's.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1879: in the first Lancashire Senior Cup [1]
- 1882-83: first FA Cup tie, a 3-2 win at Great Lever [1]
- 1884-85: five players signed from Kilmarnock Athletic [1]
- 1886-87: Bolton Charity Cup winners [1]
- 1887-88: FA Cup third round, lost 0-4 to Preston [1]
- 1888-89: The Combination; beat Crewe 5-1 in a Cup replay; Lancashire Cup wins 8-1 v Bolton and 9-4 v Burnley [1][2]
- 1889: refused by the Football Alliance [1][3]
- 1890-91: turn amateur; lose 0-12 at The Wednesday in the FA Cup [1]
- April 1892: expelled from the Lancashire Combination after losing every game; club fades out by 1893 [1]
