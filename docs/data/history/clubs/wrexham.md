## History (game text)
Formed by cricketers at the Turf Tavern in 1864, Wrexham are the oldest club in Wales and play at the Racecourse, where Wales staged its first home international. They won the first Welsh Cup in 1878, but crowd trouble at an 1883 FA Cup tie got the club expelled and it had to start again as Wrexham Olympic. In 1888-89 they won four qualifying rounds to reach the first round proper for the first time.

## Key moments to ~1895
- 1864: club formed; first game v the Prince of Wales Fire Brigade, lost 1-2 [1]
- 1876: trials for Wales's first international held at the Racecourse [1]
- 1878: win the first Welsh Cup, 1-0 v Druids [1]
- 1883: second Welsh Cup; first FA Cup tie, crowd misconduct leads to expulsion [1]
- 1884: re-formed as Wrexham Olympic [1]
- 2 Feb 1889: lose 1-3 at Swifts in the FA Cup first round [2]
- 1890: join The Combination (first game 6 Sep 1890) [1]
- 1894-95: move to the Welsh League [1]
