## 🏛️ Executive Summary

This document provides a comprehensive gap analysis of our historical football research (1888-1989). While we have excellent coverage of **major European leagues** (England, Italy, Germany, Spain), **World Cups**, and **European competitions**, we have significant gaps in **South American leagues**, **African football**, **Asian football**, **CONCACAF**, **Oceania**, **regional competitions**, and **smaller European leagues**. This analysis identifies all missing pieces and provides a roadmap for comprehensive global coverage.
