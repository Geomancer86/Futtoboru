## CM1 — The One #46, July 1992 (89%)

**Source:** `The_One_046_Jul_1992_djvu.txt` · Reviewer: **Gary Whitta** · pp. **92–93** · OCR ~lines 18328–18608

**Score note:** **89%** visible in rating column ~line 27511; sidebar OCR garbled like other The One reviews.

**Cited by:** The One #56 CM93 review (Simon Byron references Whitta’s original **89%**).

**Themes:**
- Seven years in development; 1500 players; four-player hotseat
- Transfer market depth (poach, negotiate fees/wages)
- Cups including fictional Domark trophy; Manager of Month/Year
- Presentation “threadbare” but statistics maze is the draw
- “Probably the finest example of a true enthusiast's management sim to date”
- £24.99 (per CM1_REFERENCE)

**Closing:** Demands attention; “pays handsome dividends to those willing to make the effort.”

## CM93 — Amiga Power #27, July 1993 (80%)

**Source:** `Amiga_Power_027_Jul_1993_djvu.txt` · Archive item `amiga-power-pdffromhi-000_202601` · Reviewer: **Tim Norris** · OCR ~lines 12404–12626

**Score note:** **80%** from [AMR](http://amr.abime.net/) / MobyGames; AP rating box not legible in OCR (same pattern as AC062 / TONE056).

**Themes:**
- Sequel to CM1; 37 listed new features; ~15 min setup for full game
- No match highlights, animation, or action — management/tactics/planning only
- Transfer market, injuries, board feedback, teleprinter-style Saturday results
- vs Graham Taylor's Soccer Challenge (has action) and Premier Manager (less depth)
- “Domark get back on top form with an off-the-pitch footy sim”

**Closing:** “Excellent management sim… well worth considering if you haven't got either of them.”

## CM93 — CU Amiga #40, June 1993 (84%)

**Source:** `CU_Amiga_040_Jun_1993_djvu.txt` · Reviewer: **Mark Patterson** · Page **66** · OCR ~lines 21797–22075

**Sub-scores (OCR):** Graphics 55% · Sound 80% · Instability 83% · Playability 83%

**Summary line:** “An unattractive but extremely absorbing football strategy game.” — **OVERALL 84%**

**Themes:**
- Real Premier League + European squads; reserve teams with vague ratings (“promising”, “fair”)
- Deep scout search; tactical setup per player position and formation
- Text-heavy presentation; Ian Wright digitised pic among grounds
- Annoyance: weekly transfer list for entire league shown

**Opening hook:** Reviewer “normally despise[s] football management games” but CM93 “convinced me to keep playing.”

## CM93 — The One #56, May 1993 (80% score via AMR)

**Source:** `The_One_056_May_1993_djvu.txt` · Reviewer: **Simon Byron** · OCR ~lines 15050–15230

**Note:** Score sidebar garbled in OCR; **80%** from [Amiga Magazine Rack](http://amr.abime.net/review_9133).

**Cites missing source:** Gary Whitta reviewed original CM in **July** at **89%** — hunt `SRC-MAG-TONE-CM1-JUL` in ledger.

**Themes:**
- Sequel vs original: ~36 “new features” but same structure/menu
- **Slowness** — constant disk loads between matches
- Forced full-league results / transfer news (should be optional)
- Veterans should not rebuy at ~£25; newcomers may enjoy depth if patient

**Verdict tone:** “Good game underneath” but design flaws “cannot be forgiven in a sequel”; thumbs to twiddle.
