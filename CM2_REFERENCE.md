## 1. Identity & commercial context

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Developer** | Sports Interactive (first game as registered company entity) |
| **Publisher** | Domark → **Eidos** (Eidos from CM2 onward for mainline) |
| **Release** | 22 Sep 1995 (PC DOS/Win95); Amiga port 1997 (Sterling Games — cut features) |
| **Platforms** | PC primary; Amiga reduced |
| **Engine** | **CM2 engine** (text) — runs through **CM97/98** |
| **Career limit** | Seasons simulate to ~**2025** (MobyGames) |

### Commercial significance

- First CM under **Eidos** publishing chain (Domark acquisition legacy).  
- Established annual/update business model leading to CM97/98, CM96/97 data packs.  
- **CD-ROM edition:** Optional **audio commentary** (Clive Tyldesley) — first voiced CM ✅.

## 4. Core gameplay systems

### 4.1 vs CM93/94 improvements

- More realistic **contracts & transfers**  
- **Bosman ruling** in later CM2 updates  
- Expanded **tactics** depth  
- Improved **player knowledge / scouting** (precursor to fog of war)  
- **Manager/coach data files**

### 4.2 Training

- MobyGames tags **character development / training** — basic schedules vs CM3 overhaul

### 4.3 Board & cups

- Board expectations  
- Full domestic + European cups for selected nation

### 4.4 International

- Present on **PC**; cut on Amiga port

### 4.5 Multiplayer

- **Hotseat** (carried from CM1)

### 4.6 Editor

- **No built-in editor** in base CM2 — arrives **CM97/98**

| **CM Norge** | Norway |
| **Guy Roux Manager** (1993, Ubisoft/Intelek) | French league variant |

### 5.3 CM2 European Leagues (1996)

**Official expansion CD** — landmark for global CM.

| SKU | Leagues included | Season mix |
|-----|------------------|------------|
| **European Leagues (combined CD)** | Italy, Spain, Germany, Holland, Belgium, France | Mixed 95/96 and 96/97 per league ⚠️ |
| **Spanish, Belgian & Dutch** | 3 leagues | Split release variant |
| **French, German & Italian** | 3 leagues | Split release variant |

**Install model:** Each league = **separate folder** on hard disk; often **CD required in drive** (LaserLok copy protection) — major community pain point.

**Community fix:** [cm2fix.com](http://pferrie.host22.com/misc/cm2fix.zip) (Peter Ferrie) — bypass LaserLok for DOSBox; documented on [VOGONS](https://www.vogons.org/viewtopic.php?t=33813).

**Guides:** [Champman Fans — CM2 European Leagues](https://champmanfans.wordpress.com/cm2-european-leagues/); [Champman0102.net CM2 Italian thread](https://champman0102.net/viewtopic.php?t=4151).

### 5.4 Standalone foreign league installs (CM2 base required)

Community references separate installers:

- **CM2_ITA** — Italian league (95/96 data — Lentini at Milan era)  
- **CM2_SPA, CM2_GER, CM2_HOL, CM2_BEL, CM2_FRA** — per-country folders  

Italian league rules: **3 foreign players on pitch** ⚠️ community memory.

## 12. Futtoboru relevance

CM2 teaches:

- **One league system per save** early model — Futtoboru correctly scopes to 1888 FL only.  
- **Expansion = separate data packs** — analogue to Futtoboru `mods/seasons/{id}/`.  
- **Copy protection → community patches** — lesson for mod loader stability.  
- **Season completes with champion** — Futtoboru must match this CM1/CM2 baseline.
