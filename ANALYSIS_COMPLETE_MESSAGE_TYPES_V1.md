# Complete Message Types Analysis v1.0

**Date:** 2025-12-14  
**Research:** Football Manager, PC Fútbol, FIFA Manager, and other football tycoon/simulator games  
**Goal:** Document ALL message types that should exist in the inbox system

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The inbox is the **primary interface** where players spend most of their time when advancing days forward. It should contain **comprehensive, timely, and actionable** information about all game events. This document catalogs every message type found in major football management games.

---

## 1. RESEARCH: Football Manager Series

### 1.1 Core Message Categories (FM Traditional)

#### Match Messages
- **Pre-Match Briefing**: Tactical advice, opposition analysis, team news
- **Match Preview**: Upcoming match details, team selection reminders
- **Match Result**: Full match report, goals, cards, attendance
- **Post-Match Analysis**: Player ratings, tactical analysis, media reaction
- **Match Postponed**: Weather, fixture changes, rescheduling
- **Match Abandoned**: Rare events, crowd trouble, etc.

#### Transfer & Contract Messages
- **Transfer Offer Received**: Other clubs making offers for your players
- **Transfer Offer Made**: Your offers to other clubs
- **Transfer Negotiation Update**: Progress on transfer talks
- **Transfer Completed**: Player signed/left
- **Contract Expiry Warning**: Player contract ending soon
- **Contract Negotiation**: Player wants new contract
- **Contract Offer**: You've offered a contract
- **Contract Rejected**: Player rejected your offer
- **Loan Offer**: Loan deals for players
- **Release Clause Triggered**: Another club activated release clause

#### Injury & Medical Messages
- **Injury Report**: Player injured in training/match
- **Injury Update**: Recovery progress update
- **Player Returned from Injury**: Player available again
- **Medical Assessment**: Detailed injury diagnosis
- **Suspension**: Player suspended (cards, disciplinary)

#### Training Messages
- **Training Report**: Weekly/monthly training summary
- **Player Progress**: Individual player improvement
- **Training Injury**: Injury during training
- **Training Performance**: Best/worst performers

#### Scouting Messages
- **Scout Report**: New player scouted
- **Scout Update**: Updated information on scouted player
- **Opposition Scout Report**: Analysis of upcoming opponent
- **Scout Recommendation**: Scout suggests signing a player

#### Board & Finance Messages
- **Board Meeting**: Quarterly/annual board review
- **Financial Update**: Budget, revenue, expenses
- **Transfer Budget Changed**: Board adjusted budget
- **Wage Budget Warning**: Approaching wage limit
- **Stadium Expansion**: Board approved expansion
- **Facility Upgrade**: Training/youth facility improvements
- **Board Confidence**: Board satisfaction update
- **Job Security**: Board warning about performance

#### Media Messages
- **Press Conference**: Invitation to press conference
- **Media Question**: Journalist asking questions
- **News Article**: Media coverage of your club
- **Rumour**: Transfer rumours, speculation
- **Media Reaction**: Press reaction to match/decision

#### Player Messages
- **Player Concern**: Player unhappy about something
- **Player Request**: Player wants to leave, new contract, etc.
- **Player Morale Update**: Team morale changes
- **Player Interaction**: Response to your conversation
- **Disciplinary Issue**: Player misconduct
- **Player Praise**: Player happy with something

#### Competition Messages
- **League Welcome**: Welcome to new league/season
- **Cup Draw**: Cup draw results
- **Cup Round Progression**: Advanced to next round
- **Cup Elimination**: Knocked out of cup
- **Fixture Release**: League fixtures published
- **Fixture Change**: Match rescheduled
- **League Standings Update**: Position change notification
- **Relegation Warning**: Approaching relegation zone
- **Promotion Chance**: Promotion possibility
- **Title Race Update**: Championship contention
- **Competition Prize**: Prize money awarded

#### Authority & Association Messages
- **Rule Change**: New competition rules
- **Important Dates**: Key dates announced
- **Transfer Window**: Transfer window opening/closing
- **Registration Deadline**: Squad registration deadline
- **Disciplinary Hearing**: FA/Authority hearing
- **Fine**: Club/player fined
- **Ban**: Club/player banned

#### Staff Messages
- **Staff Contract**: Staff contract expiring
- **Staff Offer**: Job offer to staff member
- **Staff Resignation**: Staff member leaving
- **Staff Recommendation**: Staff suggests signing player/staff

#### Youth & Academy Messages
- **Youth Intake**: New youth players available
- **Youth Player Progress**: Youth player development
- **Youth Player Ready**: Youth player ready for first team

#### Miscellaneous Messages
- **Award**: Player/staff won award
- **Record Broken**: Club/player record achieved
- **Milestone**: Career milestone reached
- **Fan Reaction**: Fan feedback
- **Social Media**: Social media mentions

## 5. MESSAGE PRIORITY LEVELS

### Urgent (Red Indicator)
- Match starting soon
- Contract expiring today
- Transfer deadline
- Injury to key player
- Board warning

### Normal (Default)
- Most messages
- League announcements
- Match results
- Training reports

### Low (Grayed Out)
- Historical information
- General news
- Non-critical updates

## 7. MESSAGE CONTENT TEMPLATES

### League Welcome Template
```
Title: "Welcome to the [League Name]"
Category: LEAGUE
Priority: NORMAL

Content:
"Congratulations! Your club, [Club Name], has been accepted into the [League Name] for the [Season] season.

The league consists of [Number] clubs, and you will play each team twice (home and away) throughout the season.

The season begins on [Start Date] and concludes on [End Date].

Good luck!"
```

### Cup Draw Template
```
Title: "[Cup Name] [Round] Draw"
Category: CUP
Priority: NORMAL

Content:
"The draw for the [Round] of the [Cup Name] has been completed.

Your club, [Club Name], has been drawn against [Opponent Name].

The match will be played on [Date] at [Venue].

[Additional draw results if showing full draw]"
```

### Match Result Template
```
Title: "Match Result: [Home] [Score] - [Score] [Away]"
Category: MATCH
Priority: NORMAL

Content:
"[Home Club] [Home Score] - [Away Score] [Away Club]
[Date] | [Venue] | Attendance: [Number]

Goals:
[Home Scorers]
[Away Scorers]

[Match Summary]
[Key Events]"
```

## 10. USER EXPERIENCE CONSIDERATIONS

### Message Overload Prevention
- Group related messages (e.g., all match results together)
- Allow "Mark all as read" for category
- Auto-delete old messages (optional)
- Message archiving

### Message Importance
- Visual indicators (color, icon)
- Priority sorting
- Unread count badge
- Urgent message highlighting

### Message Actions
- Link to relevant screens
- Quick actions (e.g., "View Match", "View Player")
- Response options (for player concerns, etc.)
